Direct vs Regular Plans

The 1% fee that quietly eats your wealth.

About this calculator

Direct and regular plans of a mutual fund hold the same investments. The regular plan includes a distributor commission, so its returns are lower by that fee every year. This calculator shows the long-term gap.

Common questions

What is the difference between direct and regular plans?

They own the same portfolio. Regular plans pay a distributor commission and have a higher expense ratio.

How much does the expense ratio matter?

Small yearly fees compound against you. A 1% gap can cost lakhs over 20 years on a monthly SIP.

Should I move from regular to direct?

Switching is a redemption and may attract tax and exit load, so check first.
