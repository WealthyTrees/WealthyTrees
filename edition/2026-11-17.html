India's Wholesale Inflation Jumps to 12.29% in October

India's wholesale price inflation accelerated significantly to 12.29% in October, driven largely by surging fuel and power prices. This marks the second straight month of double-digit wholesale inflation readings for the country. Higher energy costs ripple through manufacturing and transport sectors, putting broad pressure on input costs across the economy.

RBI Cancels Registration Certificates of 49 NBFCs

The Reserve Bank of India has exercised its statutory powers to cancel the certificates of registration for 49 non-banking financial companies. Separately, 13 other NBFCs and one housing finance company voluntarily surrendered their licenses back to the banking regulator. PAGE 5 · SPROUT’S LESSON

What is a circuit breaker?

A pause button for the market on very wild days.

THE ONE-LINE VERSION Circuit breakers stop trading for a while when prices move too far, too fast.

Learn this properly, free → Share this paper on WhatsApp
1

Single stocks have price bands

A stock can only move a set percentage in a day.

2

Index circuit breakers exist too

If the Nifty or Sensex falls 10%, 15% or 20%, trading halts.

3

The pause cools things down

It gives everyone time to read the news.

4

It is rare

Most days never come close.
