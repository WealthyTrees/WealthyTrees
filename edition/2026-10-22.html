THE ONE-LINE VERSION When you read “crude up”, read: India's costs went up today.

Learn this properly, free →
1

India imports most of its crude

We pay for it in dollars. There is no local tap to open.

2

The price jumps

A costlier barrel raises India's import bill overnight.

3

Fuel and transport get costlier

Trucks, flights and factories burn fuel, so everyday prices follow.

4

Profits squeeze, stocks wobble

Companies spend more and earn less, and the Nifty feels it.
