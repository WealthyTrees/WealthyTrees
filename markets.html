How to read this chart

  • Each candle is one day. The body spans the open and close. The thin lines (wicks) show the day’s high and low. Green means it closed above its open; red means below.
  • Volume bars show how much traded. A big move on low volume is a weaker signal than the same move on high volume.
  • Trend: a series of higher highs and higher lows is an uptrend. Lower highs and lower lows is a downtrend.
  • Try it: use the symbol box to switch to a stock you know, then change the interval from daily to weekly and notice how the picture calms down.
  • Remember: charts describe the past. They do not predict the future.

Stock screener

How to read the screener

  • Market cap is the company’s total value. It tells you size, not quality.
  • P/E is price divided by earnings. Compare it with companies in the same sector, never across sectors.
  • Change % shows today’s move. One day says little. Look at weeks and months.
  • Sort, then ask why. If a stock tops the list, find the news behind it before drawing conclusions.
