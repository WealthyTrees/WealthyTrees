PAGE 5 · SPROUT’S LESSON

What is the rupee, and why does it move?

The rupee goes up and down against the dollar every day. Here is why.

THE ONE-LINE VERSION More dollars coming in means a stronger rupee.

Learn this properly, free → Share this paper on WhatsApp
1

It is a price

USD/INR is the price of one dollar in rupees.

2

Dollars in, rupee up

When foreign money flows into India, more buyers want rupees.

3

Dollars out, rupee down

Costly oil or foreign selling means more demand for dollars.

4

Why you care

A weaker rupee makes imports, travel and foreign education costlier.
