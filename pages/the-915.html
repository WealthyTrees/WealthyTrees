Sensex vs Nifty: what is the difference?

Two numbers, one story. What are they?

1

Both are baskets

Each index tracks a group of big companies to show how the market did.

2

Sensex has 30

The BSE's index of 30 large companies.

3

Nifty has 50

The NSE's index of 50 large companies.

4

They move together

They are mostly similar, so one tells you the other's story.

THE ONE-LINE VERSION Sensex and Nifty are two scorecards for the same big companies.
