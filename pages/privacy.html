Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

WealthyTrees (wealthytrees.online) is an education website. We collect as little as possible.

What we collect

Newsletter: if you subscribe, we receive your email address through our newsletter provider, Buttondown. We use it only to send the daily brief and related updates. You can unsubscribe from any email.

Your device: the site stores your theme choice and course progress in your own browser (local storage). This stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Analytics: we use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand which pages readers find useful. It collects anonymised usage data — which pages you visit, how long you stay, your approximate location (city level), and your device and browser type. It does not collect your name, email or financial details, and we do not use it to identify individual visitors. You can block it with your browser’s tracking protection or an ad blocker.

Technical data: our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes standard request data (such as IP address) to deliver and protect the site.

Ask Sprout: when you ask Sprout a question, your question is sent to our AI provider (Google Gemini) so it can answer. We do not attach your name or email to it.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal data. We do not ask for bank, card, PAN or Aadhaar details. Never send them to us.

Third parties

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and market data from external providers. Analytics is provided by Google Analytics. Links to other websites are governed by their own policies. If we show ads in future, ad partners may use cookies, and we will explain this here first.

Your rights (India, DPDP Act 2023)

If you are in India, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 gives you the right to ask what personal data we hold about you, to ask us to correct it, and to ask us to delete it. To make a request, or to raise a privacy complaint, use our Contact page and we will respond. You can also unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time from any email we send.

Your choices

To remove your email or ask what we hold, use our Contact page.

Questions about this policy? See our Contact page. Also see our Terms of Use.
