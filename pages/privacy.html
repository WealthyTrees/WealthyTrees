Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

WealthyTrees (wealthytrees.online) is an education website. We collect as little as possible.

What we collect

Newsletter: if you subscribe, we receive your email address through our newsletter provider, Buttondown. We use it only to send the daily brief and related updates. You can unsubscribe from any email.

Your device: the site stores your theme choice and course progress in your own browser (local storage). This stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Technical data: our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes standard request data (such as IP address) to deliver and protect the site. If we turn on analytics, we will use a privacy-friendly tool and update this page.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal data. We do not ask for bank, card, PAN or Aadhaar details. Never send them to us.

Third parties

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and market data from external providers. Links to other websites are governed by their own policies. If we show ads in future, ad partners may use cookies, and we will explain this here first.

Your choices

To remove your email or ask what we hold, write to priyansharora@wealthytrees.online.
