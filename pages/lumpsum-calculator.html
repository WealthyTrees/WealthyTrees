Lumpsum Calculator

One payment. Let time do the work.

About this calculator

A lumpsum is a one-time investment. This calculator compounds it yearly at the return you choose, shows the Rule of 72 doubling time and compares it with a SIP of the same total.

Common questions

What is a lumpsum investment?

Investing a large amount in one go, instead of in monthly instalments.

What is the Rule of 72?

Divide 72 by the yearly return to estimate how many years your money takes to double.

Is lumpsum better than SIP?

A lumpsum gets more time in the market. A SIP spreads risk and fits monthly income.
