About

WealthyTrees brings you the day’s stock and commodity market news in short, clear summaries. No noise, no tips, no speculation. Just what happened, why it matters, and what you can learn from it.

Built for students and new investors who want to understand the markets, not chase them.

Summaries are written with AI assistance and link to their sources. Please verify before relying on them.

Founder’s story

Hi, I’m Priyansh, a finance student. I got tired of market news that needs a dictionary: headlines full of terms nobody had explained to me.

So I started WealthyTrees with a simple idea: learn the concept first, then read the headline. Every day I want the news to be clear enough that a student can follow it, and every lesson to respect your pace.

It is a learning project, not a tip service. If you have feedback or questions, write to me at priyansharora@wealthytrees.online.
