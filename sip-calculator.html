Why a SIP works

A SIP turns investing into a habit. You buy more units when prices are low and fewer when they are high, so you do not have to guess the "right time". The real power is time: try moving the years slider from 10 to 20 and watch how much of the final value is gains, not your own money. Then try a 10% yearly step-up to see what a rising income can do.

Questions

What is a SIP?

A Systematic Investment Plan invests a fixed amount in a mutual fund at regular intervals, usually monthly.

Are SIP returns guaranteed?

No. The number above is an illustration based on the return you type in. Markets can fall, and past returns do not promise future ones.

What is a step-up SIP?

A SIP whose monthly amount rises by a fixed percentage every year. Even a small step-up can add a lot over 15 to 20 years.

Why show inflation?

Prices rise over time, so a big future number buys less. The inflation line shows roughly what the final value is worth in today's money.
