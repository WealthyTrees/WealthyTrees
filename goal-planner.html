Pick a goal. We tell you the monthly SIP.

About this calculator

Type the amount you want and the years you have. The planner works backwards using the SIP formula and tells you the monthly amount needed, and what it becomes after inflation.

Common questions

How much SIP for 1 crore?

It depends on the years and return. At 12% a year, about Rs 43,000 a month over 10 years or about Rs 10,000 a month over 20 years.

Why does starting early help?

More months of compounding means a smaller monthly amount for the same goal.

Should I include inflation?

Yes. A goal of Rs 1 crore in 20 years buys much less than today, so plan for a larger goal.
